New: pin notes. Use the "Oben anheften" switch when writing or in an opened note. Pinned notes stay at the top, tap the pin to release it.
Better: the bar under each note now uses the same scale for all notes. Little time left means a short bar, three months is full.
New: choose how notes are shown. In the menu under "Darstellung" you pick list or tiles. The tile view shows notes in two staggered columns, each as tall as its content.
New: coloured notes, bright and strong. The colour is stored encrypted like the text.
New: appearance (System, Light, Dark) and colours (wallpaper or Chiffre green) can be set. Tabs sit at the bottom in a bar with icons.
