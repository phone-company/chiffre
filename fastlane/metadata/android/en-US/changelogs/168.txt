New: "Without password" (choose it during setup or under Security). Chiffre then opens without asking; notes stay encrypted, the key is kept in the Android Keystore. This protects against copied files, not against someone holding your unlocked phone. Backups get their own backup password. Switching back to a password works at any time. Repeat with a custom interval, e.g. every 2 days. Long lists: shorter tiles, the cursor stays visible while typing.
